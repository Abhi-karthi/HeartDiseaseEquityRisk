⚠️ Medical Disclaimer

This tool is designed strictly for educational, demonstrative, and advocacy purposes to highlight algorithmic equity gaps in healthcare. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider or cardiologist regarding medical decisions.

Datasets & Methodology

This application utilizes a simulated deterministic algorithm. Risk weights are modeled to reflect established clinical frameworks, including the ACC/AHA Pooled Cohort Equations (PCE) and the AHA PREVENT™ equations (which incorporate social deprivation indices). Population assumptions are drawn from historical parameters seen in the NHANES and ARIC datasets.
